This document outlines the corporate travel and expense policies for employees traveling on official business. All employees must adhere strictly to these guidelines to ensure timely reimbursement and compliance with financial regulations. Expenses incurred outside of these policies will not be reimbursed without prior written approval from a department head. Employees are expected to submit their expense reports within 14 days of returning from business travel.

Hotel Allowances: When booking accommodations for corporate travel, employees must observe the following daily maximum allowances. For travel within the United States, the maximum daily allowance for a hotel is 250 USD per night. For travel to Japan, the maximum daily allowance is 35,000 JPY per night. Accommodations should be booked through the approved corporate travel portal whenever possible to ensure negotiated rates are applied.

Currency Policy: All international expenses must be automatically converted to Thai Baht (THB) using the historical exchange rate of the transaction date. No manual user prompt is required for this conversion. For all internal auditing and automatic currency conversions to THB, the system must strictly use the fixed corporate exchange rates: 1 USD = 35 THB, and 100 JPY = 24 THB.

Meal and Incidental Expenses (M&IE): Employees are provided a daily per diem for meals and incidentals during corporate travel. The standard daily M&IE rate is 75 USD for international travel and 50 USD for domestic travel. Receipts are not strictly required for M&IE if claiming the per diem rate, but itemized receipts are mandatory for any individual expense exceeding 25 USD, regardless of the category.
